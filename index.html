Methodology

Methodology and limits (click to open)
  • Scores are 0-100 percentile ranks against the other companies shown, so they are relative. "Overall" = growth 20%, quality 20%, balance sheet 10%, value 20%, sentiment 15%, technical 15%.
  • Sentiment = analyst rating, target-price upside, short interest, 90-day revisions to full-year EPS estimates, and share of the last 4 quarters that beat EPS estimates.
  • Risk = market (beta, volatility, drawdown) 30%, financial (FCF, debt, liquidity) 25%, valuation 30%, crowding (short interest, stretched price, RSI) 15%, plus manual event-risk points listed in the hover.
  • Manual inputs: peer groups, AI-exposure ratings (1-5) and event risks are judgments, not company-reported data.
  • Data: Yahoo Finance, which mirrors filings but can have gaps or errors; fiscal calendars differ by company (quarter-end dates are shown). Data coverage % shows how many of 19 key inputs were available.
  • Stress periods are actual price moves, not forecasts. The de-rating scenario shows the price change if the forward PE fell to the peer-group median, with earnings unchanged.
  • Not captured: segment-level AI revenue, news sentiment, insider trades, legal and regulatory events beyond the manual list. Scores have not been backtested (point-in-time fundamentals are not freely available); the track record below tests them going forward instead.
